question: The main point is to get an answer from the community, in the form of help, info, or a recommendation.
analysis: Makes a claim and backs it with something checkable, like a price, a date, a number, a specific game detail, or a quote.
hot_take: Makes a claim or complaint with no checkable support. Rants land here.
share: The writer tells about something that happened to them or how they feel, without arguing a claim beyond their own experience and without asking for an answer.
